# Feature brief template

Use this artifact before implementation.

```text
Title
Goal
Actors
Current behavior (with repository evidence)
Expected behavior
Business rules
Edge cases and failure behavior
Permissions, scope, ownership, stage and maker/checker
Data, persistence, migration and audit impact
UI, RTL, responsive and accessibility impact
Acceptance criteria (observable and testable)
Files likely in scope
Files explicitly out of scope
Open product questions
Risk level: low / medium / high and why
```

An acceptance criterion must describe an observable result, including denied or failure behavior when relevant. Do not use “works correctly” or “secure” without a testable condition.
